# Lesson 01: Blinking LED

**Goal:** Understand how to properly connect an LED, read resistor values, and learn the internal structure of a breadboard.

### Components Required:
- 1 x Elegoo Uno R3
- 1 x 5mm Red LED
- 1 x 220Ω Resistor
- 1 x 1kΩ Resistor
- 1 x 10kΩ Resistor
- 2 x M-M Jumper Wires
- 1 x Breadboard

### Key Takeaways:
- **Breadboard:** Extremely useful for rapid prototyping without soldering.
- **LEDs:** Directional components (diodes). Connecting in reverse blocks current and prevents lighting up.
- **Resistors:** Learned the importance of using a current-limiting resistor in series with the LED to prevent burning it out due to high current.
- **Color Codes:** Learned how to read the resistance value using the colored bands on the body.

### Challenges & Fixes:
- **Issue:** The circuit didn't work at first because wires were connected incorrectly on the breadboard.
- **Fix:** Studied how the internal metal strips are connected inside the breadboard (rows vs. power rails) and rewired the circuit accordingly.
